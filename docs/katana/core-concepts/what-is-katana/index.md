# What Is Katana Network?

Katana is an onchain financial markets company that operates a Layer 2 blockchain. It owns the infrastructure behind its core markets, so the revenue they generate supports the network.

## What Katana Owns and Operates

- **Katana Swap:** a decentralized exchange built on Sushi's deployment on Katana, for trading tokens. See [Katana Swap](../core-apps/sushi.md).
- **Katana Perps:** a perpetual futures trading platform built by the IDEX team, which Katana acquired. See [Katana Perps](../katana-perps.md).
- [**Vault Bridge**](../vault-bridge.md): deploys the capital behind select bridged assets into yield strategies on Ethereum, generating protocol revenue.
- [**Chain-Owned Liquidity**](../chain-owned-liquidity.md): uses network revenue to build and maintain liquidity across Katana's core markets.

Together, these products create an economic model where financial activity generates revenue that supports liquidity, network growth, and KAT staking.

## What Makes Katana Different

- **Revenue-driven tokenomics:** instead of relying mainly on token emissions, Katana uses a share of the revenue from its trading venues, Vault Bridge, and sequencer fees to buy KAT on the open market. 100% of that KAT is compounded into avKAT. See [How KAT Staking Works](../../katana-staking.md).
- **Infrastructure ownership:** by owning its core trading venues and infrastructure, Katana keeps revenue that would otherwise flow to third-party operators, so more of the value created on the network supports its long-term growth.
- **Chain-Owned Liquidity:** network revenue builds and maintains liquidity across a curated set of core financial applications, reducing fragmentation, deepening markets, and improving trading efficiency.
- **Productive bridged assets:** through Vault Bridge, the capital behind select bridged assets earns yield on Ethereum, adding a revenue source beyond trading and transaction fees.

## What You Can Use Katana For

- **Staking:** stake KAT into avKAT to take part in protocol revenue. Rewards compound automatically every epoch, with nothing to claim or restake.
- **Trading:** swap tokens on Katana Swap or trade perpetual futures on Katana Perps.
- **Lending and borrowing:** lend, borrow and earn through curated vaults on Morpho, Katana's lending partner. See [Morpho](../core-apps/morpho.md).
- **Productive assets:** assets moved to Katana through Vault Bridge stay productive. The capital behind them earns yield on Ethereum, and that revenue funds KAT buybacks for avKAT stakers and incentives across Katana's markets.

## How Katana Is Built

[**Agglayer & Tech Stack**](../cdk-opgeth.md): Katana's Layer 2 uses zero-knowledge proofs to settle to Ethereum and connects natively to Agglayer, so users and assets can move between Katana and the broader Ethereum ecosystem.
