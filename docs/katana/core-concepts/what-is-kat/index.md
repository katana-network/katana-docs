# KAT Token: What It Is and How It Works

KAT is the native token of Katana. You can hold and trade it like any other token, and you can stake it to receive **avKAT**, the staked form of KAT.

Staking is built around real protocol revenue. A share of the revenue from Katana Swap, Katana Perps, Vault Bridge and sequencer fees is used to buy KAT on the open market, and 100% of that KAT is compounded into avKAT.

## The Two Forms of KAT

| | What it is | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **KAT** | The token you hold and trade | Holding, trading, and staking |
| **avKAT** | Staked KAT, with bought-back KAT compounded in every epoch | Powered by Katana's revenue, with nothing to claim or restake |

For a closer look at each form, see [Understanding the KAT Token Ecosystem](../../how-to/kat-token-overview.md).

## Key Facts

- **Fixed supply:** 10 billion KAT. No more can ever be created.
- **Launched:** KAT became transferable and launched on exchanges on 18 March 2026.
- **No venture capital investors:** no KAT was allocated to VC investors, and no group unlocks ahead of users.
- **Revenue-funded staking:** staking rewards come from KAT bought back with protocol revenue, not from new token emissions.

For the full allocation and unlock schedule, see [Tokenomics & Schedules](tokenomics.md).

!!! info "vKAT was sunset on November 17, 2026"
    vKAT staking and gauge voting have ended. If you still hold vKAT, you can [convert it to avKAT](../../how-to/kat-convert-vkat-to-avkat.md) at any time, with no deadline.
