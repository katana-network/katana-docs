<div class="hero-left" style="max-width: 85%; margin: 0 auto; padding: 20px; text-align: left;">
   <p class="hero-subtext"><a href="https://katana.network/">Katana</a> is an onchain financial markets company that operates a Layer 2 blockchain, designed to bring trading, liquidity, and yield together in one network. Unlike networks that rely mainly on third-party applications to generate economic activity, Katana owns and operates the infrastructure behind its core financial markets: Katana Swap for trading tokens, Katana Perps for perpetual futures, and <a href="/katana/core-concepts/vault-bridge/">Vault Bridge</a>, which keeps bridged assets earning yield on Ethereum.</p>
   <p class="hero-subtext">Because Katana owns these venues, the revenue they generate stays with the network. A share of revenue from trading, bridging, and sequencer fees funds open-market KAT buybacks for <a href="/katana/katana-staking/">avKAT stakers</a>, and network revenue also builds <a href="/katana/core-concepts/chain-owned-liquidity/">Chain-Owned Liquidity</a> across Katana's core markets. For users, the goal is simple: deeper liquidity, productive assets, and yield backed by real revenue.</p>
   <p class="hero-subtext">Katana uses <a href="https://agglayer.dev/">Agglayer</a> for interoperability and ZK proofs to secure state transitions, making it easier for users and assets to move between Katana and the broader Ethereum ecosystem.</p>
</p>
